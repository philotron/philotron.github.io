VoxFallbacks is an annotated dataset of 3,030 naturally occurring German fallback utterances from a smartwatch voice assistant. It accompanies the EMNLP 2026 Industry Track paper, *Not All Fallbacks Are Failures: Understanding and Recovering from Fallbacks in Mobile Voice Assistants*.

[Link to GitHub Repository](https://github.com/philotron/VoxFallbacks)
